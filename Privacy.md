​Welcome to EarnMaster Bot ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with our Telegram bot (@EarnMasters_bot).
​1. Information We Collect
​To provide you with our earning and referral services, we collect limited information provided directly through Telegram:
​Telegram User ID: A unique numerical identifier assigned to you by Telegram to create and manage your account balance.
​Basic Profile Data: Your Telegram username, first name, and last name (if available).
​Activity & Transaction Data: Records of completed tasks, daily check-ins, referral counts, and withdrawal history within the bot.
​Note: We do NOT store your personal payment credentials (such as bank PINs or sensitive passwords).
​2. How We Use Your Information
​We use the collected information solely for operating and improving the bot services:
​To maintain your account, track balance, and process daily bonuses.
​To verify completed tasks and manage the referral reward system.
​To process payout/withdrawal requests securely.
​To prevent fraud, abuse, multiple accounts per user, and automated spamming.
​3. Data Sharing & Security
​Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to external third parties.
​Security Measures: We use standard encryption and secure cloud servers (e.g., Render databases) to protect your account data from unauthorized access.
​4. User Control & Data Retention
​Your data is stored as long as you use the bot to ensure your rewards and balances remain accurate.
​You can request account reset or data deletion by contacting our support team via the /help command inside the bot.
​5. Changes to This Policy
​We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our bot functionality or legal standards. Any updates will be announced directly within the bot.
​6. Contact Us
​If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our admin through the /help command in @EarnMasters_bot.
